How the radar scores

implied value = TVL × market multiple × growth factor × age factor

    The multiple is the market's own: the median market-cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in this snapshot, per category where that category has at least three traded peers, shrunk toward the market-wide median. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100%. Age is how long the protocol has run with no token. No protocol name is ever special-cased.

      Reading the board

      • Rank 1 is the most valuable claim, not the biggest TVL: a smaller protocol growing fast can outrank a larger flat one.
      • “Records no token” is what the snapshot says, not proof a protocol has none: a protocol on the list may still have a token its record does not carry.
      • The watchlist is a floor, never a ceiling. The board carries all 40 watchlist protocols plus every other pre-token protocol the snapshot ranks, down to the smallest.
      • Farm steps are a starting plan, costed in dollars and days, built from each protocol's own category, chain, size and age — not a promise of a reward.
